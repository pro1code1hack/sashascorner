Café Ops · Sasha’s Corner, Dundee · screen wireframes

Stock you can
read at a glance

Ten screens, drawn at real size, annotated with the field behind every number and where it comes from in the database. Every figure shown is a genuine value from cafeops api-fixtures — nothing is invented.

Who it is for

A twenty-year-old who makes the coffee, not an operations analyst. She opens this on a phone in the fridge doorway and on a laptop twice a week. So: plain words over vocabulary, one obvious thing to do per screen, and the reasoning folded away until she taps for it.

What does not get simplified is honesty. An estimate still says it is an estimate, a missing number stays missing rather than becoming zero, and a figure the app worked out is never allowed to look like one somebody counted.

How a number tells you what kind of number it is

Large, in the estimate column. The app worked this out. A counted figure never appears here.

12.0 on 19 Sept

A counted figure is never printed bare — it always carries the word counted and its date.

£1.51

Dotted underline: built from an estimated price. Marked typographically, never in colour — 280 of 298 costs are estimates, so an alarm on all of it is an alarm on nothing.

The look

Light, after two years of near-black. Fraunces for headings, Public Sans for everything read, IBM Plex Mono for every figure so columns line up. Green and amber differ in lightness as well as hue, so they survive a colour-blind reader and a photocopy.
